The soul of Cambodia is found on two wheels.

SiemRide is a local guide crew running bicycle and motorbike tours off the tour-bus routes, and putting part of every ride back into the community that raised us.

Giving Back

Ride with purpose

5% of every profit we earn is donated directly to a local rural English school.

We're more than a tour company, we're part of this community. Every booking helps fund classroom supplies, teacher support, and school activities for kids growing up along the routes we ride.

About Us

Experience the soul of Cambodia

At SiemRide, we believe the true essence of Cambodia isn't found from the window of a tour bus, or on the crowded paths of the main temples. It's found in the cool morning breeze as you navigate quiet village backroads, and in the warm smiles of the people you meet along the way.

Founded by locals who are deeply passionate about our home, SiemRide was born from a simple idea: exploration should be intimate, authentic, and impactful. We specialise in curated bicycle and motorbike tours that connect travellers with the hidden wonders of Siem Reap.

“We don't ride a script, we ride the roads we'd take our own friends down.” The SiemRide guide team

Our Mission

To transform the way visitors experience Cambodia by providing immersive, eco-conscious, and adventurous journeys, and to foster genuine connections between our guests and the local communities they ride through.

Our Vision

A future for tourism where adventure and responsibility go hand-in-hand. By choosing to ride different, our guests help us prove that travel can preserve heritage, respect the environment, and empower the next generation.

Every Ride Gives Back

Book a tour with us and you're not just seeing Cambodia, you're funding a classroom for the kids along the way.
